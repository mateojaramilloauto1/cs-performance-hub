1. Show Rate

DefinitionThe share of scheduled appointments that resulted in a physical customer visit (check-in at the branch).
Source columnshow_rate — stored as decimal (e.g. 0.7293 = 72.9%). Multiply × 100 for display.
Individual countrySourced directly from the physical check-in or inspection system.
Europe (weighted)Weighted average using nb_booking_confirmation_created as the weight proxy. This is an approximation because the true Show Rate denominator (total appointments) is not available in the dataset.
Europe (unweighted)Simple average of the 9 country Show Rates. No volume weighting applied.
The true Show Rate denominator is not available in the aggregated dataset. The Europe Show Rate is a proxy. Treat European-level comparisons as directional.

2. Booking Confirmation (BC) Metrics

A Booking Confirmation (BC) is a task assigned to a Customer Service agent to call a customer after they book an appointment — to confirm attendance, reschedule, or handle cancellations.

BC CreatedNumber of BC tasks created. nb_booking_confirmation_created. Used as the main volume proxy for appointment activity.
BC DoneBC tasks completed (call made, outcome recorded). nb_booking_confirmation_done
% BC Tasks DoneShare of BC tasks completed, out of tasks not cancelled. nb_booking_confirmation_done / (nb_booking_confirmation_created − nb_booking_confirmation_cancelled). Ranges 63–83% across countries.
BC Task Cancel RateShare of BC tasks cancelled (not appointment cancellations). nb_booking_confirmation_cancelled / nb_booking_confirmation_created. Note: this is a BC task-level metric, not an appointment-level cancellation rate.
BC Open / PendingBCs not yet resolved. nb_booking_confirmation_created − nb_booking_confirmation_done − nb_booking_confirmation_cancelled. Always ≥ 1; elevated values may indicate capacity constraints.

3. Cancelled Appointment Volume

DefinitionTotal number of appointments cancelled by customers. This is a raw volume — not a rate.
Source columnappts_cancelled
Cancellation timingSub-columns show when the cancellation occurred relative to the appointment time:
ColumnLabel in dashboardMeaning
appts_cancelled_before_appt_more_30mBefore appt >30 minCustomer cancelled >30 minutes before the appointment
appts_cancelled_before_appt_less_30mBefore appt <30 minLast-minute cancellation <30 min before
appts_cancelled_after_appt_less_30mAfter appt <30 minCancellation recorded within 30 min after appointment time
appts_cancelled_after_appt_more_30m_samedayAfter appt >30 min (same day)Cancellation recorded >30 min after, same day
appts_cancelled_after_appt_from_1dayAfter appt 1+ dayCancellation recorded 1 or more days after the appointment

Missing values in cancellation timing sub-columns are treated as zero. The parent column appts_cancelled has no missing values.

4. Priority Score (0–100)

Composite score to rank countries by urgency. Higher = needs more attention. Score components:

ComponentMax ptsCalculation
BC Volume25Percentile rank of BC Created across 9 countries × 25. High-volume markets carry more risk.
Show Rate gap25If SR is below EU average: proportional to gap (5 pp below = max 25 pts). If above EU, 0 pts.
Show Rate decline20If SR fell vs comparison: proportional to magnitude (5 pp decline = max 20 pts).
% BC Tasks Done decline15If % BC Tasks Done fell vs comparison: proportional to magnitude (5 pp decline = max 15 pts).
Cancelled Vol increase10If cancelled volume rose vs comparison: proportional relative to median country volume.
BC Pending backlog5Percentile rank of BC Pending × 5.
CategoryScore rangeRecommended action
Critical75–100Immediate review required. Multiple risk factors compounding.
High50–74Close monitoring. Investigate primary driver.
Medium25–49Watch for further deterioration. No immediate action required.
Low0–24Within normal range.

5. Excluded Metrics

Total Appointment Volume is not shown. The column appts_nu_cancelled cannot be used as "appointments not cancelled" because Show Rate mathematically exceeds appts_nu_cancelled / (appts_nu_cancelled + appts_cancelled) in every single row. The true total appointment volume denominator is unknown.

Appointment Cancellation Rate is not shown for the same reason: the denominator (total appointments) cannot be validated.

The appts_nu_cancelled_* columns are not used in any business insights until their meaning is confirmed.

6. Comparison Mode

Prev periodDeltas compare current period to the immediately previous period (week-on-week or month-on-month).
4-period avgDeltas compare current period to the average of the previous 4 periods. Smooths out week-to-week noise and highlights structural trends.

7. Interpretation Caution

Insights are directional and based on aggregated country-level data. They identify potential performance drivers but do not prove causality. The correlation between % BC Tasks Done and Show Rate is directional (n=9 countries) and does not constitute statistical proof. Use these signals to guide investigation, not as definitive conclusions.
